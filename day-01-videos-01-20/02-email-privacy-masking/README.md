## Requirements

1. Trim and lowercase the email.
2. Extract the username and domain into separate variables.
3. Keep the first two and last two characters of the username visible.
4. Replace every character between them with `*`, preserving the number hidden.
5. Leave the domain intact.

## Expected output

```text

Masked: mo*******mr@example.com
Domain: example.com
```

## Constraints

Do not assume a fixed `@` index or hardcode the number of stars. Assume a username of at least four characters.
